# XPS maintenance

- Hermes intentionally has full access to this dedicated machine.
- Its 1Password service account is read-only and scoped to **Agent Access**.
  Do not broaden access or request the user's 1Password account password.
- Private application/SMS contents and Hermes transcripts belong only in
  Hermes's context under the user's chosen ZDR provider policy. Do not fetch
  authenticated page text, screenshots, records, or transcripts into maintenance
  contexts; validate using service health and nonsecret status signals.
